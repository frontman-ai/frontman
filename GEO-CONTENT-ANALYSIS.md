## Content Quality Issues

### 1. Recommendations outrun first-hand evidence

**Passage:** The quick answer, product recommendations, and final decision rules call options the best fit based on documented capabilities.

**Issue:** Qualification makes these claims honest, but AI systems have stronger reason to cite a measured comparison than a documentation synthesis.

**Rewrite after testing:**

> We ran the same checkout failure through all four options on July 16, 2026. Chrome DevTools MCP was the only option in this test that returned the disabled DOM state, failed request body, Lighthouse result, and source-mapped console stack in one workflow. Playwright MCP completed the cross-browser flow with fewer interaction retries, but did not identify framework source ownership without repository search.

Only publish that form with real results and artifacts.

### 2. Readability is below target

**Metric:** Approximate Flesch Reading Ease is 49 versus target 60–70.

**Issue:** Sentences are short, but specialized terms accumulate without compact definitions. This is acceptable for technical readers but less extractable for general AI answers.

The article now defines accessibility snapshots, source mapping, and isolated profiles at first use. Remaining difficulty comes primarily from unavoidable product names and technical terminology rather than long sentence structure.

### 3. Some one-sentence paragraphs are less citable alone

**Examples:** Short transition and definition paragraphs throughout the opening and browser-access taxonomy.

**Issue:** Most are useful emphasis, but short “answer” fragments depend on the preceding paragraph for definition.

**Suggested fix:** Keep sharp rhythm in the opening. In explanatory sections, merge each answer sentence into its supporting paragraph so extracted passages retain both claim and meaning.

- **Repeated recommendation pattern:** Quick answer, each product section, capability table, and final decision section repeat the same choice logic. Repetition is moderate, not severe.
- **Limited human voice:** The disclosure is human and specific, but the article contains no first-person account of a failed setup, surprising tool behavior, or changed conclusion after testing.
- **No original data:** Documentation synthesis is useful, but competitors can reproduce it. Original test evidence would create a defensible citation moat.
