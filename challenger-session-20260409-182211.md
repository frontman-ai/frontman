## Claims

| # | Claim | Status | Confidence | Summary |

| 1 | Logo is the right home for secondary actions (Help, Open in new window) | Refuted | High | Logo is a junk drawer; both items have better natural homes |
| 2 | "Workspace" is the correct mental model label | Refuted | High | Borrows workspace semantics without workspace substance; internal code says "task" |
| 3 | Switching + creating workspaces belong together in the left zone | Partially Verified | Medium | Spatial logic is right, but tying width to chatboxWidth makes navigation physically unstable |
| 4 | A manual URL bar belongs in the app chrome | Partially Verified | Medium | Real use case exists but it's a power-user affordance taking primary real estate |
| 5 | Device mode toggle belongs in the top bar | Refuted | High | Wrong location, wrong visual treatment for a mode, wrong usage frequency for prime real estate |
| 6 | Dot badge + bubble popup is the right nudge pattern for settings | Partially Verified | Medium | Right concept, wrong sequencing — both fire at once when they should be sequential |
| 7 | Hover-only trash icon inside a dropdown is acceptable for delete | Refuted | High | Touch-inaccessible; 4-layer interaction for a destructive action |
