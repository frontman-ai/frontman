Hey,

We just released a new version of Frontman - V5.4.0 - here are some cool things this version enables.

### Put your WordPress widgets to work

Ask Frontman to create or update block and Custom HTML widgets, or update text widgets while keeping their links clickable.

Try a request like: “Add our opening hours to this footer widget area.”

### Give pages the right template

Frontman can now assign an available page template when creating or updating a page. On supported block themes, it can also create block templates and template parts linked to your theme.

That gives you more ways to shape your site from the same conversation.

### Keep embedded content intact

Frontman now preserves embed HTML when creating or updating WordPress posts, provided your WordPress permissions allow it.

### Fewer stuck conversations

We’ve fixed disconnect handling that could leave the chat stuck. Received text stays visible, disconnected controls are disabled, and Frontman makes it clear when you need to reload.

If it’s been a while, pick one change you’ve wanted to make and give Frontman another go.
