Install for your supported stack

For Next.js, Astro, or Vite, a developer needs a running supported project and permitted workspace access. For WordPress, an administrator installs the plugin through wp-admin. Start on staging and keep backups. Sign in to Frontman, arrange service access, and connect your own AI provider. Provider access and costs are separate. See current pricing and provider setup.
